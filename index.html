Frequently Asked Questions

EDT is a mandatory 12-lesson programme for first-time learner permit holders in Ireland, delivered by an Approved Driving Instructor (ADI) and logged in your EDT log book.

Yes, you must hold a valid learner permit for Category B (car) before your first lesson. If you don't have one yet, apply through the NDLS before booking.

The RSA driving test assesses your vehicle checks, control of the car, observation, and rule-following over a real route lasting around 30-40 minutes.
