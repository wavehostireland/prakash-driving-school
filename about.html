About Me

One Instructor. Personal Attention. Real Results.

Hi, I'm Prakash - founder and sole instructor at Prakash Driving School. I set up this school because I believe learning to drive should feel calm, clear and genuinely encouraging, not stressful. Every lesson is one-to-one, so you get my full attention from the very first minute you sit behind the wheel.

Because there is only one instructor, you'll always learn with someone who already knows your progress, your nerves, and exactly what you need to work on next - no starting over with a new face every few lessons.

  • Friendly, patient teaching style suited to complete beginners
  • Safe, calm learning environment in a modern dual-control car
  • High pass rate built on structured, test-focused lessons
  • Manual and automatic lessons available across Dublin
